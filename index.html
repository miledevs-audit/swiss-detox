The short version

Swiss Detox already looks and feels like a luxury brand: a consistent gold-and-sage identity, premium photography, desktop PageSpeed 98/100, complimentary U.S. shipping, 30-day returns, and strong proof — 69 reviews averaging about 4.8★, an award and a dermatological study on 50 volunteers.

Where the sales are lost. The path to the product: SHOP opens the homepage, the homepage has no product grid or visible prices, the hero button leads to a science page with no product link — and on product pages the reviews, the trust facts and the buy button stay out of sight.

What we propose. Nothing here needs a redesign: every change reuses the current theme, its sections, fonts and sage-and-gold palette.
