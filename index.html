The short version

Swiss Detox already looks like a luxury brand, with free U.S. shipping, 30-day returns, a skin study and 69 reviews averaging above 4.8.

What we noticed. No prices on the homepage, a hero button that leads away from the products, no $25 set in the menu, and hidden reviews, trust facts and buy button on product pages.

What we propose. 13 changes, each on one screen — today on the left, after on the right.
